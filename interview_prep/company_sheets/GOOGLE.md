# Google Interview Preparation

Google often focuses on specific algorithmic patterns built around **Graphs**, **Dynamic Programming**, and **Trees**. Their interviews test your ability to take a brute-force approach and optimize it significantly.

## High Frequency Patterns at Google
1. **Graphs (DFS/BFS/Union Find):** Extremely common for their infrastructure and mapping products.
2. **Dynamic Programming:** Usually 1-D DP or string manipulations.
3. **Sliding Window:** Often mixed with Hash Maps for string/array problems.

## Recommended Practice Sequence

### 🔴 High Priority (Solve within 20 mins)
- [`Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters`](../Sliding%20Window/_must_do/Longest%20Substring%20Without%20Repeating%20Characters) (Sliding Window)
- `Word Ladder` (BFS)
- `Merge Intervals` (Arrays)

### 🟡 Medium Priority (Solve within 30 mins)
- `Course Schedule II` (Topological Sort / DFS)
- `Alien Dictionary` (Hard Topological Sort)
- `Decode Ways` (1-D DP)

### 🟢 Low Priority (Awareness)
- `Trapping Rain Water` (Two Pointers)
- `Find Median from Data Stream` (Heaps)

## Google Specific Advice
- Over-communicate your thought process. 
- Always explicitly define your Time and Space Complexity before writing a single line of code.
- State your brute-force solution verbally to prove you understand the problem before attempting the optimized `O(N)` solution.
